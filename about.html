VELTRIX is a technology-focused publication exploring artificial intelligence, robotics, computing, science and the ideas shaping the future.

What is VELTRIX?

VELTRIX is built around one simple idea: technology is changing faster than ever, and understanding that change matters.


We explore emerging technologies, explain complex ideas in an accessible way, and examine the possibilities created by innovation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make technology interesting, understandable and inspiring. VELTRIX focuses on the ideas and developments that could influence tomorrow.

What We Explore

Artificial intelligence, robotics, computing, emerging technologies, science and the future of intelligent machines.
